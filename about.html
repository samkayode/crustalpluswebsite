About Us

Crustalplus Consulting Limited is a multidisciplinary geo-services company committed to providing dependable solutions in borehole drilling, geological and geophysical surveying, mineral exploration, installation services, water treatment, and professional training. We combine modern technology with technical expertise to deliver accurate results, clean water solutions, and long-lasting projects for individuals, communities, industries, and government organizations.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, affordable, and innovative geo-solutions that ensure access to clean water, accurate subsurface information, and sustainable resource development — while maintaining professionalism, integrity, and customer satisfaction on every project.

To be a leading and trusted geoscience and water solutions company in Africa, recognized for excellence, innovation, and outstanding service delivery that positively impacts communities and industries.

Our Core Values

  • Professionalism – We work with skill, discipline, and commitment.
