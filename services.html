Our Services

Borehole Drilling & Groundwater Development

We offer domestic, industrial, agrictural, and community borehole drilling services, from site investigation to drilling, installation, and development, we ensure a sustainable water supply with long-lasting results

Geological & Geophysics Survey

We conduct subsurface investigations using modern geophysical and geological techniques for groundwater detection, engineering construction, foundation studies, environment surveys, and resource mapping.

Geotechnical Investigation / Soil Testing

At CrustalPlus Consulting Limited, we provide professional soil testing and analysis services to physical and chemical poperties of the soil. This helps in identifying the best foundation design, agrictural planning and environmental management solution.

Mineral Exploration & Resource Evaluation

Our team helps clients identify, evaluate, and assess the quantity and quality of mineral deposits using professional exploration methods for informed invesment and mining decisions

Water Treatment & Purification

We provide treatment solutions for borehole and surface water, including filtration, disinfection, iron and manganese removal, reverse osmosis, softnening, and other purification systems to ensure safe and clean water

Installation & Maintenance Services

We handle the installation and maintenance of borehole components such as submersible pumps, surface pumps, overhead tanks, control panels, and retication systems for efficient water distribution

Technical Training & Consultancy

We offer professional training, field support,and project consultancy in borehole drilling, geophysical surveying for students, organizations and institutions
